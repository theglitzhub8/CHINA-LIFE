How to play

Go places. Do things. Live.

  • 🗺️
    Move aroundTap the floor to walk. Tap Map to see the whole city, then tap a place and choose walk, metro or taxi.
  • ✨
    Do activitiesEvery venue has activities at the bottom of the screen: attend a lecture, work a shift, eat jollof, dance, swim. Each one takes game time and changes your needs.
  • 📱
    Use your phoneTap Phone for everything else: jobs, learning, food delivery, wallet, homes, rank, chat and settings.
  • ⏱
    Check TodayThe Today chip on the left shows what is due next: a meal, your shift, rent or your daily reward.
Stay alive

Look after your six needs

Hunger, energy, fun, social, hygiene and bladder drop as time passes. Keep them up and you feel great; let them hit zero and you faint.

Tips

FAQ

Questions

Is ChinaLife free?

Yes. ChinaLife is free to play in your browser and inside the Hafrik app.

Is the money real?

No. ChinaLife uses in-game yuan with no cash value. Nothing you buy, win or send in the game costs or pays real money.

Are the places real?

The cities, neighbourhoods and landmarks are inspired by real places, but the venues, prices and stories are game versions, not real services or bookings.

How do I save my progress?

Sign in with Hafrik. Your character saves automatically and loads on any device you sign in on.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Go to the same venue, chat or use voice, add each other as friends and send money.
