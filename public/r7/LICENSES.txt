R7 realism assets — all CC0 1.0 (public domain), no attribution required (credited anyway).

- anims.glb: Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" v3 (UAL1_Standard: Idle, Walk, Jog, Dance, Sitting, Idle_Talking), CC0. Fingers/scale tracks stripped, resampled.
- tex/asphalt_*: ambientCG Asphalt025C; tex/paving_*: PavingStones130; tex/brick_*: Bricks076C; tex/concrete_*: Concrete034 — ambientCG.com, CC0. 512px colour/normal, 256px AO+roughness (ORM: R=AO, G=roughness), WebP.

R8 photo facades (tex/fac_day_*.webp, tex/fac_night_*.webp): ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Facade018A/C, Facade019A/C, Facade020A/C, Facade001, Facade017 — day colour + window mask (from Metalness) and night colour,
  packed into 2x2 atlases by scripts/r8-assets/facades.mjs.
R8 crowd LOD (r7/man_lo.glb, r7/woman_lo.glb): meshoptimizer-simplified copies of the Quaternius Universal Base Characters above (CC0), made by scripts/r8-assets/people-lod.mjs.

hair.glb (R9): hairstyles, beard and eyebrows from Quaternius "Universal Base Characters" (CC0 1.0, quaternius.com), re-expressed as head-bone meshes by scripts/r9-assets/hair.mjs. Afro, curly, locs and cap shapes are generated in code (realism.js).

r7/rb/loco.glb (R9e) — walking and running motion capture from the same Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (MIT, licence below),
