How ChinaLife works

ChinaLife is a single-player life simulation. You create a foreigner, choose your start, work shifts, manage six needs, travel between districts, build skills and relationships, and improve your home.

1. Make a lifeLook → personality → dream → background → home.
2. Keep movingActivities take game time and change your needs.
3. Build statusSkills unlock better jobs, businesses and neighbourhoods.
4. Live togetherEvents, chats and other players will share the same city.
